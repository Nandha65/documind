LUMENFIELD LABS - IT SECURITY POLICY
Version 2.0, effective August 1, 2026. Policy owner: Daniel Okafor, Head of Security.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and interns who use company accounts, devices or data.

1. ACCOUNTS AND PASSWORDS
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across services.
- Store all work passwords in Vaultkeeper, the company password manager.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every company account. Administrators must use a hardware security key as their second factor.
- Never share your password or MFA codes, including with the IT team.

2. DEVICES
- Company laptops must have full-disk encryption turned on.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Operating system and browser security updates must be installed within 7 days of release.
- Personal devices may access email and Slack only, through the approved mobile apps.

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION
All company data falls into one of four levels:
- Public: approved for anyone, such as marketing pages and published documentation.
- Internal: for employees only, such as this policy, the employee handbook and meeting notes.
- Confidential: limited to the people who need it, such as contracts, financial results and salary data.
- Restricted: the most sensitive data. All customer data, including Ledgerly invoices and customer contact details, is Restricted.
Restricted data must never leave approved production systems, be copied to a laptop, or be shared over chat.

4. AI TOOLS
- Do not paste Confidential or Restricted data into any external AI tool or chatbot.
- DocuMind is approved for Public and Internal documents only. Do not add Confidential or Restricted documents to its data folder.
- New AI tools must be reviewed by the Security team before use with company data.

5. REPORTING INCIDENTS
- Report any suspected security incident within 1 hour of noticing it. Email security@lumenfield.example or post in the #sec-incidents Slack channel.
- Lost or stolen devices count as incidents and must also be reported within 1 hour, so the device can be remotely locked and wiped.
- Forward suspected phishing emails to phishing@lumenfield.example and then delete them. Do not click links or open attachments.
- The Security team responds to incident reports 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

6. ACCESS REVIEWS
Managers review their team's access to systems every quarter. Access is removed on an employee's last working day.

7. TRAINING
All staff complete IT security training during their first week and a refresher every 12 months.

Breaking this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Questions go to security@lumenfield.example.
