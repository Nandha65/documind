# Lumenfield Labs Employee Handbook

_Version 4.2, last updated July 15, 2026. Owner: People Operations (hr@lumenfield.example)._

This handbook covers the policies that apply to all full-time employees in Pune, Lisbon and remote locations. Where local law gives employees more generous terms, local law applies.

## 1. Working hours

The standard working week is 40 hours. Everyone is expected to be available during core hours, 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone, for meetings and collaboration. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

Overtime is not expected. If a deadline requires working more than 45 hours in a week, talk to your manager so the time can be given back the following week.

## 2. Hybrid and remote work

Lumenfield is a hybrid company. Employees attached to the Pune or Lisbon office work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays and may work remotely on the other days. Team leads can ask for an extra office day during planning weeks.

Working fully remote requires approval from both your manager and People Operations. Fully remote employees visit an office at least twice a year, with travel paid by the company.

You may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Tell People Operations at least two weeks before you travel so that tax and insurance rules can be checked.

## 3. Leave

### Annual leave

Every employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by June 30, after which they expire. Unused leave is not paid out, except where local law requires it.

### Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over.

### Other leave

- Parental leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver, available to all parents including adoptive parents.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for the loss of an immediate family member.
- Public holidays: each office follows its own public holiday calendar, published every December for the next year.

### How to request leave

Request all leave in the PeopleDesk portal. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days needs at least two weeks' notice and approval from your manager. Sick leave can be recorded on the day.

## 4. Expenses and business travel

Submit expense claims in PeopleDesk within 30 days of the expense, with a receipt for every item. Your manager approves claims, and approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

Travel limits:

- Meals while travelling on company business: up to $50 per day.
- Hotels: up to $180 per night. Book through the company travel portal where possible.
- Flights: economy class. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 8 hours.
- Local transport: public transport or standard taxis; no premium ride categories.

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at company events approved in advance by a director.

## 5. Equipment

New employees choose a MacBook Pro 14-inch or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or sooner if they fail. Employees also receive a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a chair, desk or monitor. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when you leave.

## 6. Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,200 per financial year for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over. You can also take 2 paid learning days per year to attend training or study. Requests go through your manager in PeopleDesk.

## 7. Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from two peers and a conversation with your manager. Promotions and salary changes are decided in the April cycle only.

## 8. Onboarding

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy for their first 60 days. During the first week you will set up your accounts and devices, complete the IT security training, and meet your team and buddy. Your manager will agree 30-, 60- and 90-day goals with you.

## 9. Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, to People Operations at hr@lumenfield.example, or anonymously through the ethics line in PeopleDesk. Reports are handled confidentially and retaliation is prohibited.
