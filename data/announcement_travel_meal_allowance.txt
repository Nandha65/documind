FINANCE ANNOUNCEMENT - Travel meal allowance increase
Posted October 20, 2026 in #announcements by Meera Shah, Finance Lead.

From November 1, 2026, the daily meal allowance for business travel increases from $50 to $75 per day. This replaces the $50 limit in section 4 of the employee handbook, which will be updated in the next handbook revision.

Other travel limits do not change: hotels remain capped at $180 per night, and flights remain economy class except for flights longer than 8 hours.

Meals on trips that took place before November 1, 2026 are reimbursed under the old $50 limit, even if the claim is submitted later.

Questions about this change go to finance@lumenfield.example.
