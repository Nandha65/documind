# Lumenfield Labs: Company Overview

_Sample data for testing DocuMind. Lumenfield Labs and every person, figure and address in these documents are fictional._

## Who we are

Lumenfield Labs is a software company founded in March 2021 by Ananya Iyer (Chief Executive Officer) and Tomás Rivera (Chief Technology Officer). The headquarters is in Pune, India, and a second office opened in Lisbon, Portugal, in September 2023. As of October 2026 the company has 140 employees: 92 in Pune, 38 in Lisbon and 10 working fully remote.

Our mission is to give small businesses the same quality of software that large enterprises take for granted.

## Products

- **Ledgerly** is an invoicing and payments product for small businesses. It launched in June 2022 and has about 3,200 paying customers. It is the company's main source of revenue.
- **Fieldnote** is a mobile app for on-site inspections (checklists, photos and signatures). It has been in private beta since May 2026, with a public launch planned for February 2027.
- **DocuMind** is an internal knowledge assistant that answers employee questions from company documents. It was built in 2026 and is used only inside the company.

## How the company runs

- The financial year runs from April 1 to March 31.
- A company-wide all-hands meeting happens on the first Monday of every month at 4:00 PM India Standard Time (11:30 AM in Lisbon).
- Teams plan work in six-week cycles, followed by a two-week cooldown for fixes and small improvements.
- Company-wide announcements are posted in the #announcements Slack channel and archived in the internal wiki.
